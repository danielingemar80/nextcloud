FIRST-TIME SETUP
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. Ensure your database(s) exist and are accessible from the cluster.
  2. Confirm your StorageClass supports ReadWriteMany (e.g. NFS, CephFS).
  3. On first boot Nextcloud will auto-install — this can take ~60 seconds.
  4. To run OCC manually:
       kubectl exec -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deploy/nextcloud-<tenant> \
         -c nextcloud -- php occ <command>
